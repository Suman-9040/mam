Dear Nibedita Mam,

I don't know how to say this properly, but I will try my best. You have been one of the most important people in my college life. Every class with you felt special, and every word you spoke taught me something new.

You were not just a teacher — you were a guide, a well-wisher, and someone I could always look up to. Whenever I was confused or low, your support and kind words gave me strength.

I know you are leaving, and honestly, it makes me sad. But I also feel happy for you, because I know you are going to do great things wherever you go. I just hope you never forget your students, especially me.

Thank you for everything, Mam. Thank you for your patience, your kindness, and your belief in me. I will always be grateful.

Wishing you all the happiness, success, and peace in the world.
